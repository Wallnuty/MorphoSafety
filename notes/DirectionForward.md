Cost shaping toward the foothold: a dense term proportional to how far inside a forbidden disc a stance foot is, not just whether. The geometry says the fix is a few centimetres per step; a binary signal can't say which direction.
The morphology question, reframed. Not "which body can cross cheaply" — the nominal one can — but "which body makes the cheap crossing learnable, or has more margin". Longer reach widens every lane; smaller feet shrink every forbidden disc. Cost in the co-design objective is the way to ask it.
